04 Mechanics

Engineering in Motion

Hover a panel to speed the mechanism and read its purpose.

Panel 01

Needle Motion

Precision needles rise within millimetre-accurate cam tracks to seize yarn and draw loops. At up to 4,000 stitches per minute, every pass is exact.

Panel 02

Yarn Feeding

Yarn is delivered at constant tension through feeders and guides, so stitch size never varies — even at enormous machine speeds.

Panel 03

Loop Formation

Yarn wraps around needles to interlock successive loops — the fundamental stitch that builds every knitted fabric.
