01 Profile

The Story

I'm Shourav Hossain, a garments pattern making expert from Dhaka, Bangladesh. I specialise in accurate, well-graded patterns for shirts, pants, and t-shirts — combining hard work with honesty and professionalism in every project I take on. I am currently studying at the Department of Textile Engineering, Northern University Bangladesh, where I continue to sharpen both craft and technical perspective.

I hold a deep love for my village home. Its peaceful surroundings, open fields, and quiet natural beauty keep me grounded — no matter where I go, that is where my mind finds its rest.
