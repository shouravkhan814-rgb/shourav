Machine Study

Circular
Knitting Machine.

A circular knitting machine forms continuous knitted fabric by feeding yarn through a circular arrangement of needles and mechanical cam systems.

CKM-01 · Studio Render — hover or tap a hotspot
01

Yarn Feeding

Yarn is delivered from cones through feeders and guides at constant, even tension into the needle zone.

02

Cam System

Fixed cam tracks guide each needle's rise and fall with millimetre precision, controlling stitch height.

03

Needle System

Hundreds of latch needles rotate in the cylinder, seizing yarn to draw interlocked loops.

04

Loop Formation

Knock-over and take-up work together so every loop is drawn to an identical size and shape.

05

Fabric Take-down

Rollers pull the finished seamless tube downward at a constant rate, matching knit production speed.

Mechanics

How Fabric
Is Formed.

The three essential movements that build every knitted fabric, shown in sequence.

Stage 01

Needle Motion

Precision needles rise within millimetre-accurate cam tracks to seize yarn and draw loops. At up to 4,000 stitches per minute, every pass is exact.

Stage 02

Yarn Feeding

Yarn is delivered at constant tension through feeders and guides, so stitch size never varies — even at enormous machine speeds.

Stage 03

Loop Formation

Yarn wraps around needles to interlock successive loops — the fundamental stitch that builds every knitted fabric.
